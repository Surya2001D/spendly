Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run Spendly. This includes:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data: information your browser sends automatically, such as your IP address and browser type.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to create and secure your account, to store and display your expenses, and to generate your spending summaries. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your data.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit the site. Google's handling of that request is covered by its own privacy policy. We do not share your account or expense data with any third party unless the law requires us to.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, email us at privacy@spendly.example.
